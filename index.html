Taichi Racing Uploader

Taichi Racing Uploader is a private, single-user tool run by Taichi Ito, an individual creator in Japan. It uploads short videos about Japanese horse racing, which Taichi makes himself, to his own YouTube channel.

What it does

  • Uploads videos that Taichi created to his own YouTube channel, with a title, description and thumbnail.
  • Adds an English title, description and English subtitles to the same video, so overseas fans can follow it.
  • Before anything is made public, it checks the text against Taichi's own content rules. Videos that fail the check stay private until Taichi reviews them.

Who can use it

Only Taichi. The tool is not offered to the public, has no sign-up, and is used only with his own Google account and his own YouTube channel.
