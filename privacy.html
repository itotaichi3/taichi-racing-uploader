Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 23, 2026

Taichi Racing Uploader ("the tool") is a private tool used only by its operator, Taichi Ito, to upload his own videos to his own YouTube channel. This policy explains what data the tool uses.

YouTube API Services

The tool uses YouTube API Services. By using the tool, users agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. The Google Privacy Policy also applies.

What data the tool accesses

  • An OAuth access token for the operator's own Google account, with permission to upload videos and manage captions on his own channel.
  • The IDs and status of videos the tool uploaded, to avoid uploading the same video twice.

The tool does not access, collect or store any data about other YouTube users or viewers, including comments, subscribers or watch history.

How the data is used

The data is used only to upload the operator's videos, titles, descriptions, thumbnails and subtitles to his own channel, and to set each video's visibility.

Where the data is stored

The OAuth token and the upload log are stored only on the operator's own computers. They are not sent anywhere except to Google's API servers.

Sharing

The tool does not share, sell or transfer any data to third parties.

Cookies

The tool does not use cookies or place any tracking on devices.

Revoking access and deleting data

Access can be revoked at any time from the Google security settings page. After access is revoked, the stored token is deleted, and the upload log is deleted within 30 days.
